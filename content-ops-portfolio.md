## Sample 2: Bilingual Content Adaptation

**English source idea:** Enterprise software adoption is moving from tool replacement to workflow redesign.

**Chinese adaptation:** 企业软件的价值不只是替代旧工具，而是重新组织工作流。对公司而言，真正重要的不是“用了什么软件”，而是团队能否通过更清晰的数据、流程和协作方式，提高决策效率。

**Content operation note:** A good translated article should not only convert language. It should adjust examples, structure, and headline logic for the target reader.

## Sample 3: Daily Content Workflow

1. Read market and technology news from reliable sources.
2. Select topics based on audience relevance, timeliness, and brand fit.
3. Build a simple fact table: source, date, core data, key quote, risk note.
4. Draft a concise article outline.
5. Review logic, wording, numbers, and tone before publishing.

## Sample 4: Content Quality Checklist

- Is the headline specific and not exaggerated?
- Are data and time references clear?
- Does the article explain why the topic matters to readers?
- Are tables easy to scan?
- Is the Chinese expression natural after translation?
- Does the content match the brand tone?
